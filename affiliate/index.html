Good to know.

Do I need a big following?+

No. There is no follower minimum, and plenty of our creators start fresh accounts. We pay for views, and on TikTok views come from the For You page, not from your follower count.

What happens if a video goes viral?+

Earnings for a single video stop at $250, which is about 333,000 views. Every other video you post earns on its own, so a big month is still a big month.

Which language should I post in?+

Use a language of the countries we serve, except English, under the current programme terms. Lingofisy launches with English, Turkish, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese. Make clear which Daily course you are showing and that AI speaking requires Lingofisy Pro. English-language content is not eligible under the current programme terms.

Why does the link have to stay in my bio for 7 days?+

That link is how we see which views came from your video. If it is not there, we cannot count them, and we cannot pay for them.

When exactly do I get paid?+

Views are counted 7 days after a video goes up, and payouts run weekly to the PayPal address on your application.

Do I have to say it is a paid partnership?+

Yes. Use your platform's paid-partnership label and follow the disclosure rules where you live. It protects you as much as it protects us.

What happens if I use bots or purchased views?+

Using bots, purchased views, automated traffic or other methods to artificially inflate views is prohibited. If we confirm that you used or commissioned these methods, you will be permanently disqualified from the Creator Program and will not be eligible to rejoin. No payout will be made for affected videos. We will explain the reason for our decision, and you may contact us if you believe it was made in error.

Can I be removed from the programme for other reasons?+

Yes. Missing paid-partnership disclosures or content that misrepresents Lingofisy can also result in removal from the programme. We will explain the reason for our decision.
