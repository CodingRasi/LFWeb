Questions, answered

Good to
know.

Still wondering if Lingofisy is right for you? Start here.

Who is Lingofisy for?+

Both ends of it. If you are starting from zero, a placement test sets your level and explanations arrive in your own language at A1 and A2. If you have studied for years and still freeze when someone speaks to you, speaking out loud to a coach who answers back is the part that unsticks you. Available in English, Turkish, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish or Portuguese.

How long are the lessons?+

Most daily activities take around 5–10 focused minutes, short enough that you will still do it tomorrow.

Can I choose what I learn?+

Yes. Every language has its own Daily course built around everyday situations, and a short placement test sets your starting level so you do not begin from scratch if you do not need to.

What is Lingofisy Pro?+

Pro is where you stop studying a language and start speaking it. You get unlimited conversations with your AI coach, your pronunciation checked as you speak, your mistakes corrected rather than just marked wrong, and feedback on word choice, grammar and pace after every session. It is the part a flashcard cannot give you, and the part that decides whether you can hold a conversation when it counts.

Is Lingofisy free?+

Yes. The free plan gives you lessons, vocabulary, grammar, quizzes, review and progress tracking, everything you need to build the habit and get the words in. Lingofisy Pro adds the speaking half: unlimited conversations with your AI coach, pronunciation checks, live corrections and feedback on how you sound. Subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play and renew automatically until you cancel.

Which languages can I learn?+

English, Turkish, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese. Each one has its own Daily course, such as Daily English or Daily German.

How does the speaking practice work?+

You talk, Fisy talks back, and your pronunciation is checked while you speak. Mistakes get corrected in the moment, and you get notes on word choice, grammar and pace when the session ends. No lesson to book, no partner to find, no one to be embarrassed in front of, and no cap on how many times you repeat a sentence until it sounds right. It needs an active Lingofisy Pro subscription.

Can I view explanations in my native language?+

Yes. At A1 and A2 you can read explanations translated into the native language you choose during onboarding.

Is Lingofisy available on iPhone and Android?+

Lingofisy is coming to both iOS and Android. The App Store and Google Play listings go live on release day.
