Ask us anything ↗
Who is Lingofisy for?+

Lingofisy is for people who want practical English they can use in everyday life, work, travel and real conversations, from beginner to advanced.

How long are the lessons?+

Most daily activities take around 5–10 focused minutes, with optional speaking practice when you have more time.

Can I choose what I learn?+

Yes. Start with a placement experience and choose General English, Business English or IELTS preparation. Your path can evolve as your goals do.

What is Lingofisy Pro?+

Lingofisy Pro gives you more room to practise, including expanded AI speaking feedback and the full learning experience.

Is Lingofisy free?+

Lingofisy has a free tier you can use without paying. Lingofisy Pro is a paid subscription that adds unlimited AI speaking practice, personalized corrections and the full learning experience. Subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play and renew automatically until you cancel.

Which English levels does Lingofisy cover?+

General English runs from CEFR level A1 to C1, Business English covers B1 to C1, and IELTS preparation is band-focused. A short placement experience sets your starting point, so you are not dropped into a beginner track by default.

How does the speaking practice work?+

You speak out loud and Fisy responds. Feedback is about clarity and expression rather than accent, so you can try a sentence, hear what landed, and say it again straight away. Unlimited speaking practice is part of Lingofisy Pro.

Can Lingofisy help me prepare for IELTS?+

Yes. The IELTS path gives structured practice across speaking, listening, reading and writing. Lingofisy is an independent language-learning service, is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the organizations that own or administer the IELTS examination, and cannot guarantee a particular band score.

Is Lingofisy available on iPhone and Android?+

Lingofisy is being built for both iOS and Android. The App Store and Google Play listings are not live yet, so the store buttons on this page are placeholders until launch.
