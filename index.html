Questions, answered

Good to
know.

Still wondering if Lingofisy is right for you? Here are the things people ask first.

Ask us anything ↗
Who is Lingofisy for?+

Lingofisy is for people who want to build language skills for everyday life, travel and real conversations. At launch, you can learn English, Turkish, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish or Portuguese.

How long are the lessons?+

Most daily activities take around 5–10 focused minutes. AI speaking practice is available separately with Lingofisy Pro.

Can I choose what I learn?+

Yes. Choose Daily English, Daily Turkish, Daily Russian, Daily German, Daily French, Daily Italian, Daily Spanish or Daily Portuguese for everyday language learning. A placement experience helps set your starting point.

What is Lingofisy Pro?+

Lingofisy Pro is a paid subscription that includes unlimited AI speaking practice and personalized speaking feedback. AI speaking is exclusively a Pro feature.

Is Lingofisy free?+

Lingofisy has a free plan with lessons, vocabulary, grammar, quizzes, review and progress tracking. The free plan does not include AI speaking access or a free speaking allowance. AI speaking requires Lingofisy Pro. Subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play and renew automatically until you cancel.

Which languages can I learn at launch?+

English, Turkish, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese. Each language has its own Daily course, such as Daily English or Daily German.

How does the speaking practice work?+

With Lingofisy Pro, you speak out loud and your AI coach responds with feedback to help you practise. AI speaking requires an active Pro subscription and is not included in the free plan.

Can I view explanations in my native language?+

Yes. At A1 and A2, you can view translations of explanations in the native language you select during onboarding.

Is Lingofisy available on iPhone and Android?+

Lingofisy is being built for both iOS and Android. The App Store and Google Play listings are not live yet, so the store buttons on this page are placeholders until launch.
